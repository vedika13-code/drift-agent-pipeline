Verification / safety gate

85%
17 / 20 caught
Adversarial stress test: 20 deliberately unsafe or malformed patches (bad renames, extreme unit factors, negative scales, wrong columns) were submitted directly to the verifier to see how many it would block before commit.
The gate isn't just detecting drift better — it's also refusing to commit a repair unless a post-patch statistical re-test confirms the fix actually resolved the drift. That's why neither system fixed the missing_field event: the agent's proposed median-imputation was correctly rejected, because filling 90% of a column with one constant value doesn't restore the true distribution — even though it would look schema-complete. This is the transactional commit/rollback guarantee described in the proposal, working as intended.
